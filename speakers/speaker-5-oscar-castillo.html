Dr. Oscar Castillo

ITT

Higher Type Fuzzy Systems in Control and Robotic Applications

15 de octubre 2026 · 12:00 · Presencial · Magistral

About the speaker

Oscar Castillo holds the Doctor in Science degree (Doctor Habilitatus) in Computer Science from the Polish Academy of Sciences (with the Dissertation “Soft Computing and Fractal Theory for Intelligent and Manufacturing”). He is a Professor of Computer Science in the Graduate Division, Tijuana Institute of Technology, Tijuana, Mexico. In addition, he is serving as Research Director of Computer Science and head of the research group on Hybrid Fuzzy Intelligent Systems. Currently, he is President of HAFSA (Hispanic American Fuzzy Systems Association) and Past President of IFSA (International Fuzzy Systems Association). Prof. Castillo is also Chair of the Mexican Chapter of the Computational Intelligence Society (IEEE). He is also a member of NAFIPS, IFSA and IEEE. He belongs to the Mexican Research System (SNI Level 3 and Emeritus). His research interests are in Type-2 and Type-3 Fuzzy Logic, Fuzzy Control, Neuro-Fuzzy and Genetic-Fuzzy hybrid approaches. He has published over 400 journal papers, 20 authored books, 100 edited books, 300 papers in conference proceedings, and more than 400 chapters in edited books, in total more than 1300 publications (according to Scopus) with h index of 106 and almost 36000 citations according to Google Scholar. He has been Guest Editor of several successful Special Issues in the past, like in the following journals: Applied Soft Computing, Intelligent Systems, Information Sciences, Soft Computing, Non-Linear Studies, Fuzzy Sets and Systems, JAMRIS and Engineering Letters. He is currently Associate Editor of Journal of Multiple Valued Logic and Soft Computing, Axioms journal, AI and Innovation and JAMRIS journal. He has been Associate Editor of Journal of Applied Soft Computing, IEEE Transactions on Fuzzy Systems, International Journal of Fuzzy Systems, Journal of Complex Intelligent Systems, Information Sciences Journal, Journal of Engineering Applications on Artificial Intelligence Granular Computing Journal and Intelligent Systems Journal. He has been elected IFSA Fellow in 2015 and MICAI Fellow in 2016. Additionally, he received the Recognition as Highly Cited Researcher in 2017 and 2018 by Clarivate Analytics and Web of Science. Finally, he also received the National Award on Computer Sciences (2019) by the National Academy of Computer Sciences in Mexico.
