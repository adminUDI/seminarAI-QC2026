Acerca del ponente

At the beginning of his Ph.D., he focused on computer science–related optimization problems, aiming to improve machine performance for processing processor-intensive computational workloads more efficiently. His doctoral and master’s research addressed resource management challenges in High Performance Computing (HPC) and distributed memory systems, emphasizing performance evaluation, load balancing, scheduling, and power management. Over time, he transitioned toward applied research, seeking to leverage computational resources efficiently to solve complex problems in material science and healthcare. Since October 2012, he has served as the Computer Science Program Coordinator at CETYS University, Tijuana, Mexico, leading research initiatives in these domains. In healthcare, his work centers on applying Natural Language Processing (NLP) and deep learning techniques to improve medical diagnosis. In material science, he develops deep learning methods for designing neutron detection materials, collaborating with renowned institutions such as the Extreme Materials Laboratory at UC San Diego (USA).
