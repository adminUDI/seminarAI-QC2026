Experimental Analysis of Quantum Error Correction Integration in Hybrid Quantum-Classical Systems for Classification with Variational Quantum Circuits

15 de octubre 2026 · 12:40 · Virtual · Ponencia

About the speaker

Jordi González is a Mexican software developer and Ph.D. Candidate, born in Mexico City in 1995. He studied Mechatronics Engineering at the Unidad Profesional Interdisciplinaria en Ingeniería y Tecnologías Avanzadas (UPIITA-IPN). Subsequently, he pursued his second university degree in Mathematics at Universidad Abierta y a Distancia de México (UNADM). Then, he completed his Master's in Software Engineering and Computer Systems at the Universidad de La Rioja. Currently, he is finishing his Ph.D. at Computer Science at Centro de Investigación en Computación (CIC-IPN), focusing on a research projects related to quantum error correction in quantum computers. J. González is a exprienced professional in software development (~10 years) and has worked as a software developer for national and international companies, including Banco de México, HMSHost, Inter-American Development Bank, Honeywell, McDonald’s and currently in Amazon as Senior System Development Engineer. His areas of interest include Software Engineering, Cloud Computing, Quantum Computing, and Topology.
