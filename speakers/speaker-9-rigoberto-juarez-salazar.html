Artificial intelligence in optical three-dimensional imaging: advances and perspectives

About the Speaker

Rigoberto Juarez Salazar received his M.Sc. (2011) and Ph.D. (2014) in Applied Physics from Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla, Mexico. He is part of the Cátedras Conacyt Program at the Faculty of Physical and Mathematical Sciences of the Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla. He has published over eighty papers in scientific journals and international conferences. He is a member of Mexico’s National System of Researchers (Level 2) and the International Society for Optics and Photonics (SPIE, USA). His research interests include three-dimensional scanning, optical metrology, visual navigation, and opto-digital image processing.
