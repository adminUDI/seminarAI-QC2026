About the speaker

Sandra S. Rosales holds a Master’s degree in Computer Science with honors from the Universidad Autónoma de Querétaro, and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from the Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana, Iztapalapa campus (UAM-I). In 2020, she was awarded first place in master's-level research by the Consejo de Ciencia y Tecnología del Estado de Querétaro (CONCYTEQ). She is currently pursuing a Ph.D. at the Centro de Investigación y Desarrollo de Tecnología Digital of the Instituto Politécnico Nacional (CITEDI-IPN), where she is advancing her academic career. Her research focuses on the design of algorithms that optimize the use of computational resources in both classical and quantum architectures. She analyzes, implements, and evaluates various algorithmic approaches to address complex problems, refining each solution according to the specific requirements of the system or application. Her primary interest lies in developing quantum algorithms that effectively leverage the capabilities of quantum computing.
