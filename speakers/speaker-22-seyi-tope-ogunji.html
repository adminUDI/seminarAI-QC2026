About the Speaker

Seyi Tope Ogunji is a doctoral researcher in Quantum Cryptography and Artificial Intelligence at the Instituto Politécnico Nacional (IPN) in Mexico, currently on academic placement at Prince of Songkla University in Thailand. He graduated with distinction in Electrical/Electronic Engineering (CGPA 3.76/4.0), earning honors including Best Graduating Student in the Faculty of Engineering and a State Award for Best Graduating Engineering Student, before completing a master’s degree cum laude on a Mexican Federal Government scholarship. His studies have been further supported by SECIHTI, five BEIFI scholarships across his master’s and PhD, and COFAA-IPN, which funds his current placement in Thailand. His research on AI-enhanced quantum cryptography has produced six peer-reviewed publications in Springer Nature and Scopus-indexed journals, and he has presented internationally across Europe and North America, including a talk at the Technical University of Munich, Germany, and has also attended QUANTICS 2026, organized by INSTICC in Porto, Portugal.
