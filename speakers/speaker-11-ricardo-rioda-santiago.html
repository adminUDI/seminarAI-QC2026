Acerca del ponente

Ricardo Rioda Santiago Sanchez earned his degree in Computer Systems Engineering from Tecnológico Nacional de México in 2024 and is currently pursuing a Master's in Engineering Sciences at the same institution. In 2019, he competed in the National Physics Olympiad, earning an honorable mention. In 2024, he took part in the Womanium Quantum Workshop and the Seminar on Artificial Intelligence and Quantum Computing, and presented research on Vision Transformers for industrial defect detection at NEO 2024. In 2025, he attended a Quantum Information Science Summer School at the University of Massachusetts Amherst and presented his research at SPIE Optics + Photonics in San Diego, California. He has also joined international AI hackathons in Paris, Tokyo, and San Francisco, applying his skills in agentic systems and applied machine learning. His interests include mathematics, physics and machine learning, and he continues deepening his expertise in AI to solve real-world challenges.
