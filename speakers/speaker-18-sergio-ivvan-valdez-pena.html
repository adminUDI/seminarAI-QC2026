Sergio Ivvan Valdez Peña

Centro de Investigación en Ciencias de Información Geoespacial, A.C.

The Bayesian operating characteristic curve for feature analysis applied to urban land cover change

October 15, 2026 · 11:00 · Virtual · Keynote

About the Speaker

S. Ivvan Valdez holds a Master's degree and a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Centro de Investigación en Matemáticas A.C. (CIMAT) and is currently a researcher within the Investigadoras e Investigadores por México programme of SECIHTI, stationed at CentroGeo in Querétaro, Mexico. His research focuses on optimization algorithms, numerical methods, and parallel computing, with applications to geospatial and engineering problems, including optimal design, time-series model fitting for pollutant and climatological variables, prediction of violent-event counts, and land-use change analysis. His publication record comprises over 100 works, with more than 45 being journal articles. He has supervised two doctoral, eleven master's, and six undergraduate theses. He has served as principal investigator on two research projects and as a collaborator on seven, and acts as a reviewer for SECIHTI-Mexico as well as for several international journals and conferences. He is a recipient of three best paper awards at international conferences and holds membership in the National System of Researchers at Level 2. His teaching experience encompasses instruction at the undergraduate, master's, and doctoral levels.
