About the Speaker

Rita Rosa Beltrán Castro is a Chemical Engineer and is currently pursuing a Master of Science degree in Digital Systems at CITEDI-IPN. Her academic work integrates engineering, artificial intelligence, and neuroscience, with a particular interest in applying deep learning techniques to medical imaging. Her graduate research focuses on the use of Vision Transformers for the early detection of Alzheimer's disease, aiming to improve the diagnostic accuracy of this condition. In 2026, she presented her scientific article, "Early Detection of Alzheimer's Disease (AD) Using Vision Transformers: A Preliminary Methodological Study," at the Tijuana International Research Congress, where she shared her findings with the academic community. Her background includes experience in machine learning, quantum mechanics, and scientific writing, which supports her interdisciplinary profile in digital systems and medical applications.
