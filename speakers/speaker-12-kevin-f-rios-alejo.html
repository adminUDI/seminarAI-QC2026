Kevin F. Rios-Alejo

UABC

Autonomous Navigation using Visual Memories: Integrating ROS, RGBD, and AI Algorithms

October 16, 2026 · 10:20 · In-person · Presentation

About the Speaker

Kevin Fabian Rios Alejo received his B.S. degree in Mechatronics Engineering from Instituto Tecnologico de Mexicali in 2024. He is currently pursuing an M.S. degree in Instrumentation ́ and Automatic Measurements in Electronics at Universidad Autonoma de Baja California ́ (UABC), with a research line centered on artificial intelligence. His current thesis project proposes an autonomous navigation system that operates with visual memories, integrating ROS, an RGB-D sensor, and artificial intelligence algorithms. In parallel, he teaches Fluid Mechanics and Research Workshop II at Instituto Tecnologico de Mexicali. His research ́ interests lie in computer vision and intelligent robotic systems, and he aims to pursue a long- term career focused on advancing computer vision applications within autonomous systems.
