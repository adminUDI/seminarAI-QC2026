[CITEDI] PROGRAM SPEAKERS COMMITTEE REGISTRATION YOUTUBE VENUE CONTACT

Welcome to the Seminar on Artificial Intelligence and Quantum Computing!

On October 16 and 17, 2025, we welcome you to join us, either in person at the Auditorium of the Center for Research and Development of Digital Technology (IPN-CITEDI) in Tijuana, Mexico, or virtually, for two days of exploration at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Quantum Computing.

This seminar is a space for everyone: students, researchers, professionals, and the simply curious. Expect engaging talks, open discussions, and the freedom to ask questions that challenge the boundaries of knowledge.

Through initiatives like this, CITEDI, with the participation of CETYS Universidad and Universidad Politécnica de Pachuca, fosters a vibrant community where ideas evolve into innovation. One of the core objectives is the training and promotion of scientific vocations among youth, encouraging the next generation to pursue discovery and research.

We believe in the power of shared knowledge to shape the future, and we hope this seminar becomes a recurring tradition that continues to inspire and connect minds across disciplines.

Below you’ll find the detailed daily schedule for the Seminar on Artificial Intelligence and Quantum Computing, taking place on October 16 and 17, 2025. Each day features a carefully curated lineup of sessions, including keynote lectures and technical presentations, covering a wide range of topics such as quantum algorithms, machine learning, and real-world AI applications.

Each image below represents a specific block of the program. Click on any image to view the full schedule for that segment. We encourage all participants to explore the sessions, engage with the speakers, and take full advantage of this opportunity to learn, connect, and be inspired. Whether attending in person or online, this schedule is designed to help you plan your experience and make the most of both days.

Afees Olamilekan — Presentation · Virtual Conference

Solving a Hamiltonian Function of Split Transmon Qubits Capacitively Coupled with a Resonator in a Cavity Quantum Electrodynamics

Friday, October 17, 2025 ⁞ 2:40 PM

Tiamiyu Afees Olamilekan is a researcher with a strong background in quantum computing and quantum information science (QIS). He holds a B.Sc. in Solid State Physics and an M.Sc. in Theoretical Physics from Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife, Nigeria. His undergraduate research focused on the design and construction of a cathode ray oscilloscope (CRO), while his M.Sc. research explored the quantization of damped harmonic oscillators using perturbation theory.

Currently, he is working on superconducting multi-qubit systems in cavity quantum electrodynamics (cQED), with research interests spanning quantum computing, quantum cryptography, and quantum communication. Tiamiyu is passionate about advancing the frontiers of quantum technologies and aims to make meaningful contributions to global quantum research efforts.
