He is currently Associate Editor of Journal of Multiple Valued Logic and Soft Computing, Axioms journal, AI and Innovation and JAMRIS journal. He has been Associate Editor of Journal of Applied Soft Computing, IEEE Transactions on Fuzzy Systems, International Journal of Fuzzy Systems, Journal of Complex Intelligent Systems, Information Sciences Journal, Journal of Engineering Applications on Artificial Intelligence Granular Computing Journal and Intelligent Systems Journal.
